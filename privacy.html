Privacy Policy

Your privacy is not an afterthought — it's our core foundation.

✅ Effective Date: June 15, 2026

1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Short Version: MusiMoon Player processes your music library and playlists 100% locally on your device. We do not track your listening habits, harvest metadata, or operate user tracking servers.

MusiMoon Player ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how media library access and audio data are handled on your device.

2. On-Device Music & Media Access

MusiMoon requests permission to access your local Apple Music Library and Device Storage (MPMediaLibrary) solely to read your audio files, song metadata (Artist, Title, Album), and artwork for local playback within the app.

  • No Cloud Uploads: Your personal music files, listening history, and playlist data never leave your device.
  • Local Storage: Custom playlists and equalizer presets are saved strictly in your device's local application sandbox.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

MusiMoon does not contain third-party tracking SDKs or cross-site ad trackers. We do not sell, rent, or trade your listening information to advertisers.

1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Short Version: Alo Translate stores profiles and conversation histories on your physical device. Translation API requests are stateless and not saved to user accounts.

Alo Translate provides AI voice interpretation with an on-device-first architecture.

2. Device Permissions & Encryption

The app requests access to the Microphone and Speech Recognition to transcribe and translate your voice. All personal user profiles are encrypted locally using iOS Keychain Services.

3. Stateless Translation Requests

Audio snippets sent for translation processing are encrypted in-transit (HTTPS/TLS) and are stateless — no audio or text is linked to your personal identity or retained for AI model training.

1. Local Network Permission & TV Discovery

Short Version: PhoneToTV uses your Local Wi-Fi Network strictly to discover DLNA and Smart TV receivers. We do not log, inspect, or store your streamed media contents.

PhoneToTV uses iOS NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription to detect Smart TVs (Samsung Tizen, LG WebOS, Sony, etc.) on your home network.

2. Media Streaming & Privacy

Screen mirroring and video streaming signals are transmitted directly over your local Wi-Fi router from your phone to your TV screen. No video content or browsing activity is transmitted to external cloud servers.

1. Moonray LLC Umbrella Privacy Policy

All applications built by Moonray LLC follow our strict "Privacy by Design" framework. We minimize data collection, prioritize local on-device processing, and provide user-controlled data deletion options.
