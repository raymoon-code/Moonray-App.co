MusiMoon — Music & Playlist Features

What is MusiMoon Player? ▼
MusiMoon is an iOS offline and online music player designed for high-fidelity playback, seamless playlist creation, background audio streaming, and personal music organization. Available on the Apple App Store.
How do I import songs and create custom playlists? ▼
Tap the "+" button in the Playlists tab to create a new collection. You can add songs from your local device storage, Apple Music library, or cloud imports. Drag and drop to rearrange your play queue at any time.
Does MusiMoon support background playback and lock screen controls? ▼
Yes! MusiMoon integrates with iOS Now Playing controls and AirPlay. You can pause, skip tracks, adjust volume, and view album artwork directly from your iPhone lock screen, Control Center, or connected Bluetooth devices.
Is an internet connection required to play my songs? ▼
No. Any music files saved locally on your device can be played 100% offline without any cellular or Wi-Fi data connection.

MusiMoon — Troubleshooting & Audio Settings

Audio stops when locking the screen or switching apps. ▼
Ensure Background App Refresh and Media Library permissions are enabled in iPhone Settings → MusiMoon. Also make sure Low Power Mode is not restricting background audio playback.
How do I back up or restore my custom playlists? ▼
Playlists are automatically included in your standard iOS iCloud Backup. When setting up a new iPhone, restoring from iCloud will preserve all your MusiMoon playlists and custom equalizer presets.

Alo Translate — Real-Time Voice Translation

How does Alo Translate work? ▼
Alo Translate is an AI-powered real-time voice interpreter. Select your source and target languages, tap the microphone button, and speak naturally. The app transcribes your voice, translates it with AI, and reads the translation out loud.
Which languages are supported? ▼
Alo Translate supports over 40 languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Arabic, Portuguese, and more.
Where is my translation history stored? ▼
All personal profiles and conversation logs are stored securely on your physical device using iOS Keychain Services. We do not store your private conversation history on cloud databases.
Microphone or speech recognition is not responding. ▼
Go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and ensure Alo Translate is enabled. Also verify Speech Recognition permissions are granted.

PhoneToTV — Screen Mirroring & DLNA Casting

How do I connect my iPhone to my Smart TV with PhoneToTV? ▼
Ensure both your iPhone and Smart TV (Samsung Tizen, LG WebOS, Sony Android TV, or DLNA receiver) are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network. Open PhoneToTV, select your TV from the auto-discovery list, and tap Start Mirroring or Stream Video.
Why is my Smart TV not appearing in the discovery list? ▼
1) Verify Local Network permission is enabled in iOS Settings → PhoneToTV. 2) Ensure your TV is turned on and connected to the same Wi-Fi subnet. 3) Restart your Wi-Fi router if device discovery is blocked by router AP isolation.
Can I cast web videos directly to my TV? ▼
Yes! PhoneToTV includes an in-app Web Video Sniffer. Navigate to any video website using the built-in browser, and PhoneToTV will automatically extract the video link and stream it directly to your Smart TV in high definition.
